# CompassionGPT: AI for Moral Circle Expansion

**Author:** Suvarna Ahire  
**Live Application:** Deployed on Vercel  
**Research Paper:** *AI for Compassion: Investigating the Potential and Limits of Large Language Models in Expanding the Human Moral Circle*

---

## Overview

CompassionGPT is an interactive web platform and research prototype designed to explore how Large Language Models (LLMs) can function as "moral prosthetics." Powered by Groq's high-speed LPU inference engine (`llama-3.3-70b-versatile` and `llama-3.1-8b-instant`), the platform helps users:

1. **Socratic Ethical Chat:** Engage in reflective, non-confrontational moral discussions.
2. **Perspective AI Simulator:** Generate scientifically grounded first-person lived experiences for any sentient creature.
3. **Moral Circle Quiz:** Assess their Moral Expansiveness Index with an interactive animated SVG diagram and AI analysis.
4. **Daily Compassion Challenges:** Receive personalized micro-habits tailored to their lifestyle.
5. **Research Monograph:** Access and download the complete academic PDF and Word white papers.
