Peer-Grade Research Monograph 5-Page APA Specification

AI for Compassion: Investigating the Potential and Limits of Large Language Models in Expanding the Human Moral Circle

Author: Suvarna Ahire (AI Engineer & Ethics Researcher) • Citations: 45 Peer-Reviewed Academic Sources

1. Abstract & Theoretical Grounding

The expansion of humanity's moral circle—the perimeter of entities deemed worthy of ethical consideration—represents one of the most profound markers of philosophical progress. While evolutionary psychology and historical sociology confirm that human empathy evolved primarily to protect kin and in-group tribal members, modern suffering-focused axiology requires extending consideration to all sentient beings. This monograph examines whether Large Language Models (LLMs) and conversational Socratic agents can systematically mitigate psychic numbing, dismantle speciesist cognitive biases, and expand the human moral circle.

2. Section 8.1: Core Design Principles for CompassionGPT

8.1.1 Non-Judgmental Communication

Avoids guilt-based messaging; validates cognitive friction to prevent psychological reactance and defensive hostility.

8.1.2 Evidence-Based Responses

Grounds assertions in veterinary neurobiology (limbic systems, C-fibers, cortisol) rather than emotional rhetoric.

8.1.3 Respect for User Autonomy

Presents balanced viewpoints and open-ended Socratic questions, encouraging self-directed ethical inquiry.

8.1.4 Inclusivity Across All Sentience

Extends moral consideration across humans, farmed livestock, companion animals, wild animals, and future synthetic minds.
