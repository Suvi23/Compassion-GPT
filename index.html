📚 About This Research

CompassionGPT is based on research into AI-assisted moral circle expansion. The concept of the "expanding moral circle" was popularized by philosopher Peter Singer, suggesting that throughout history, humans have gradually expanded the boundary of beings whose interests we consider morally relevant.

This tool uses Socratic dialogue — thoughtful questioning rather than lecturing — to help users explore and potentially expand their own moral boundaries.

About CompassionGPT

AI for Compassion: Expanding the Moral Circle

🎯 Project Overview

CompassionGPT is a research prototype exploring how AI-powered Socratic dialogue can help people expand their moral circle — the boundary of beings whose well-being they consider morally relevant. Through guided conversation, perspective-taking exercises, and thoughtful questioning, the system aims to foster deeper empathy and compassion.

🧠 The Socratic Method

Rather than lecturing users about compassion, CompassionGPT uses the Socratic method — asking probing questions that help users discover insights through their own reasoning. This approach is grounded in research showing that self-discovered insights lead to deeper and more lasting attitude changes.
